Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06 (Asia/Shanghai)

What we process

The FlatNest converter runs entirely in your browser with client-side JavaScript. Your JSON input is not uploaded to our servers for conversion. We do not have a database of your pastes.

Hosting & logs

The static site may be hosted on Surge (or similar). The host may collect standard web logs (IP, user-agent, requested URL) as part of serving files. We do not intentionally collect personal document contents.

Contact & payments

If you email us for Pro early access, we will use your email only to reply and deliver a license. When Creem/Stripe checkout is enabled, payment data is handled by that provider under their terms — we only receive what they share for fulfillment (e.g. email, purchase status).
